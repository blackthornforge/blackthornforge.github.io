About the game

Ashes of Thornfield is made by Blackthorn Forge, a small independent studio. It is a classic isometric hack-and-slash built for phones: short sessions when you have five minutes, a long endgame when you have more.

The game is free. It is supported by optional rewarded ads, for example to revive your character. You never have to watch an ad to keep playing.

  • Normal, Nightmare and Hell difficulty, plus the Ashen Reach endgame
  • Character level up to 99, skills up to level 25 with gear
  • Online rooms, same-Wi-Fi play and a PvP Arena
