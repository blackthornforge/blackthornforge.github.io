Ashes of Thornfield

A dark fantasy isometric hack-and-slash action RPG for Android. Fast combat, deep loot and a long endgame. Play offline or with friends.

Get it on Google Play
  • Four classes: Warrior, Mage (Fire, Cold, Lightning, Poison), Necromancer and Fighter.
  • Skills that evolve every 5 levels.
  • 25+ bosses, Normal, Nightmare and Hell difficulty, and the Ashen Reach endgame.
  • Normal, Magic, Rare, Set and Unique loot, gems and sockets.
  • Co-op up to 8 players, online or on the same Wi-Fi.
  • Free to play. Optional rewarded ads only. No account needed.
