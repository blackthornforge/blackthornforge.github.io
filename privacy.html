Privacy Policy – Ashes of Thornfield

Last updated: November 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Ashes of Thornfield (package com.phamtinh.ashesofthornfield, "the Game") handles information. The Game is made by Blackthorn Forge, an independent developer ("we", "us").

1. Information we collect ourselves

We do not sell personal data, and we do not build profiles of players.

  • Game progress (characters, items, settings, language, volume) is saved only on your device, in the Game's private storage. We cannot access it. Uninstalling the Game deletes it.
  • The Game has no account, no login and no in-app purchases.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game offers optional rewarded ads (for example, to revive your character or unlock a character slot). Ads are provided by Google AdMob. When an ad is requested or shown, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers,
  • general device and app information (device model, operating system, language, app version),
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • ad interaction data (ads shown, clicks, whether an ad was completed).

Google uses this data to deliver, measure and personalise ads, and to prevent fraud, under the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices:

  • Ads are optional; you never have to watch one to keep playing.
  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the Game asks for your consent (through Google's consent tool) before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the in-game Menu → Ad Privacy Settings.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

3. Playing together

  • Same Wi-Fi: gameplay data (character name, class, level, position and actions) is sent directly between the devices on your local network. It does not pass through our servers.
  • Online rooms: when you create or join an online room, gameplay data is forwarded between players through our relay server. The relay does not store gameplay data. Like most internet services, it keeps short-lived technical logs (such as IP addresses and connection times) for security and abuse prevention, and keeps them only for a limited time.

4. Google Play services

The Game uses the official Google Play In-App Updates library to check whether a newer version is available on Google Play. This check is handled by the Google Play Store app under Google's Privacy Policy. The Game also checks its own installation signature on the device to detect modified copies; this information is not sent anywhere.

5. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes

We may update this policy when the Game changes. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest version.

7. Contact

Questions: ashesofthornfield@outlook.com

  • Chúng tôi không bán dữ liệu cá nhân. Tiến trình chơi chỉ lưu trên máy bạn. Game không có tài khoản, không đăng nhập, không mua bán trong game.
  • Quảng cáo (Google AdMob): game có quảng cáo có thưởng không bắt buộc. Khi tải / hiện quảng cáo, Google có thể thu thập mã quảng cáo và mã thiết bị khác, thông tin thiết bị và ứng dụng, địa chỉ IP (vị trí gần đúng), dữ liệu tương tác với quảng cáo, theo Chính sách quyền riêng tư của Google.

  • Game dùng thư viện Google Play In-App Updates để kiểm tra bản mới, và tự kiểm tra chữ ký cài đặt trên máy (không gửi đi đâu).
